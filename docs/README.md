## Scope and honesty notes

- The project is a **research prototype**, not a production fraud system.
- All reported metrics are **retrospective** on a reserved IEEE-CIS test split
  that has been reused across phases.
- The tuned model's training-only 5-fold cross-validation is the cleanest
  generalization estimate in the repository.
- The IEEE-CIS dataset is **not included**. The trained model and preprocessor
  are provided for research and education only.
- No business impact, revenue saving, or production deployment is claimed.
